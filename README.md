# neto-homepage-preview
Static visual homepage concept for Neto Designs review only; no checkout or live store changes

## Image sources

- Original logo retained from this preview.
- The hoodie, crewneck and full-zip sweatshirt photographs come from the corresponding public Neto Designs product pages.
- The plain T-shirt and chef jacket come from the existing Neto Designs preview asset library. The casacca (ND001456), camice (ND001674) and apron (ND001925) are archived Neto catalogue images. Those references identify the photos and do not verify inventory.
- Workshop and LAB interiors come from the existing Neto Designs photo library.
- Finished projects and paired LAB transformations are reserved for verified photographs.

## Additional preview photography

- Lavori realizzati uses three completed embroidery examples from the Neto Designs Dropbox photo archive: a chef jacket pocket, a staff shirt and a work apron.
- Hotel and school tiles use the archived Neto catalogue imagery for reception wear (ND001671) and a hospitality-school chef jacket.
- Industry and polo tiles use the Rossini A0010601 and Gildan GI64800 supplier catalogue photographs, respectively. These photos illustrate categories.
- The LAB comparison is reserved for a verified pair showing the same garment.
